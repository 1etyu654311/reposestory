## Why Should You Start Investing?

Investing isn't a luxury — it's a necessity in today's world. With rising inflation eroding purchasing power, it's crucial that your money works for you.

## Fundamental Concepts

### What Is Investing?

Investing means putting your money into assets that grow in value over time. It differs from saving, which is simply storing money.

### Types of Investments

1. **Stocks**: Ownership shares in companies
2. **Bonds**: Loans to governments or corporations
3. **Real Estate**: Property investments
4. **Index Funds**: Diversified baskets of stocks

## Steps to Start Investing

### Step 1: Define Your Goals

Before you begin, ask yourself:
- What is my investment objective?
- What is my expected timeline?
- How much risk can I tolerate?

### Step 2: Build an Emergency Fund

Before investing, ensure you have savings covering 3-6 months of expenses.

### Step 3: Start Small

You don't need a large sum to begin. Even $100 per month can accumulate significantly over time.

## Golden Rules for Beginner Investors

- **Diversify**: Don't put all your eggs in one basket
- **Think Long-Term**: Investing is a marathon, not a sprint
- **Learn Continuously**: Read and follow financial news
- **Don't Follow the Herd**: Make decisions based on research and analysis

## Conclusion

Investing is a journey that starts with a single step. Start today, learn from your mistakes, and keep growing.
