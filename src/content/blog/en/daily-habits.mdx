## The Power of Small Habits

Big change doesn't require giant leaps. It's enough to build small, consistent habits. As James Clear says in "Atomic Habits": **improving by 1% every day means a 37x improvement in a year**.

## Morning Habits

### 1. Wake Up Early

Waking up early gives you quiet time for planning and reflection. Start by waking up just 30 minutes before your usual time.

### 2. Meditation or Deep Breathing

Dedicate 10 minutes to meditation or deep breathing. This helps:

- Reduce stress and anxiety
- Improve focus and attention
- Boost energy levels

### 3. Journaling

Write down 3 things you're grateful for every morning. This simple habit radically changes your perspective on life.

## Work Habits

### The Simple Technique: 25/5

- Work for 25 minutes with full focus
- Take a 5-minute break
- After 4 rounds, take a longer break (15-30 minutes)

### Identify Your Top 3 Tasks

Every morning, identify just 3 tasks that must be done today. Focus on them and ignore everything else.

## Evening Habits

1. **Screen Off**: At least one hour before bed
2. **Read**: Even 10 pages daily = 12 books per year
3. **Plan Tomorrow**: Write tomorrow's tasks before bed

## Conclusion

Don't wait for perfect conditions. Start today with one simple habit, and watch your life transform gradually.
